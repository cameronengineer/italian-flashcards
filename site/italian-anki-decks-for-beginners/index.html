Italian Anki Decks for Beginners: Choosing a First Deck

By Cameron, creator of this collection.

These Italian flashcards are separate downloads, not a sequence you need to finish in order. If you are starting Italian, choose one vocabulary deck, check its sample cards and add specialised practice when you need it.

A1 vocabulary or Fluent Forever?

The Italian A1 Anki deck follows the CILS A1 word list. It covers vocabulary for family, food, shopping and getting around. Choose it if you want vocabulary organised around a named language level.

The Fluent Forever vocabulary deck is based on Gabriel Wyner’s core word list. Its topics include home, body, food and time. Choose it if you prefer an everyday topic-based starting point. The collection’s deck name refers to the source list; the current downloadable note count is shown on its page.

Common words occur in both decks. Start with one rather than importing both and assuming every card will be new.

Which CILS level should I download?

The A2, B1 and B2 decks include lower-level vocabulary. Choose the level you are currently studying. Importing all four can repeat words and increase your review queue without adding much new material. The CILS levels page compares them side by side.

A deck’s label describes its vocabulary source, not a promise that finishing the deck gives you that proficiency level. Listening, reading, grammar and conversation still matter.

When to add numbers and verbs

The Italian numbers deck focuses on easily confused forms, including teens and tens. Use it if recognising a number takes longer than you want.

For verbs, start with a tense you are learning in your course or reading. The presente deck practises forms such as vedo, “I see”. The verb conjugation overview lists the other tenses and imperative persons. You do not need to import all of them together.

Check the card before importing

Every deck page includes a real front-and-back sample, a short preview of its contents, card counts and file size. Vocabulary cards generally ask for both recognition and recall; verb and noun-phrase drills ask for the Italian. A note can produce more than one card, so note and card counts differ.

The pictures, voices and card text are AI-assisted. Listen to the sample and check whether the prompts suit you. If an entry seems wrong, consult a dictionary and edit or suspend it in Anki.

A first study session

  1. Download one vocabulary deck.
  2. Open the .apkg file in Anki and check the import summary.
  3. Choose a modest daily new-card limit, then adjust it as you see your review workload.
  4. Try to answer before revealing the back. Listen to the recording and say the Italian aloud.
