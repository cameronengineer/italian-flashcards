What is CILS?

CILS (Certificazione di Italiano come Lingua Straniera) is a certificate in Italian as a foreign language from the Università per Stranieri di Siena. It covers all six levels of the Common European Framework, A1 to C2; the B1 and B2 exams are called UNO-B1 and DUE-B2. These decks cover vocabulary for A1 to B2.

Which level should I download?

  • Level A1 · Beginner: 482 words for shopping, family, food and getting around.
  • Level A2 · Elementary: Everyday conversation: work, travel, health, plans. 563 new words, plus all of A1.
  • Level B1 · Intermediate: Situations, stories and opinions. 502 new words, plus A1 and A2.
  • Level B2 · Upper-intermediate: Vocabulary for films, news and discussion. 541 new words, plus A1 to B1.

Choose the level you are studying now. Each deck repeats the levels below it, so moving up later means importing a larger deck that includes words you have already studied.

Preparing for CILS B1 Cittadinanza

CILS also has a separate B1 Cittadinanza exam for people applying for Italian citizenship, with its own format. The B1 deck can help with vocabulary, but it does not practise the listening, reading, writing or speaking tasks. Check the current requirements with the office handling your application.

What the decks do not cover

These are independent study decks, not official CILS material, and they are not affiliated with the Università per Stranieri di Siena. They practise vocabulary only: grammar, listening and writing need other study. Text, pictures and voices are AI-assisted and may contain mistakes. Sources and limitations.
